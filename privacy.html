出租资料，保存在自己的设备上

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 11 日。开发者：Wenjie Lu。联系邮箱：luwenjie.nz@gmail.com。

RentDesk 租屋管家是一款离线房源、租约、租金和提醒记录工具。应用不设账号，不提供云同步，不接入广告、跟踪或第三方分析软件包，也不将房源、租客、租金、照片或使用记录上传给开发者。

应用在本机处理哪些资料

  • 你输入的房源名称、地址、城市、区域、房型、卧室、人数、参考租金与备注，用于整理房源和生成文案草稿。
  • 你输入的租客姓名、电话、邮箱、成人与儿童人数、租约日期和周期租金，用于登记租约和计算应收记录。应用不读取通讯录或定位。
  • 你登记的收款金额、日期和备注，用于显示收款与余额。应用不连接银行，不执行支付。
  • 你主动选择的房屋照片在本机解码、缩小并重新编码后保存。应用不自动上传图片；默认封面是随应用提供的通用 AI 示意图。iOS 版仅通过系统“文件”选择你明确选中的 JPEG、PNG 或 WebP，不申请相机或相册访问权限，也不提供拍照入口。相册照片可先使用系统“存储到文件”，再导入。
  • 每日时间、租金提前天数、声音设置和单独提醒内容，供本机安排系统通知。每日与租金提醒使用通用提示；你填写的单独提醒内容可能出现在锁屏通知中。可在 iOS 设置中关闭通知、声音或通知预览。

保存、备份与删除

iOS 版在应用专属目录保存资料，并使用系统文件保护；保存时保留上一份有效记录用于故障保护。本机资料目录排除在应用自动备份之外。各设备资料独立，没有 iCloud 同步，请通过“设置与备份”定期导出 JSON，迁移设备或删除应用前尤其需要备份。

JSON 完整备份包含房源、租客联系方式、租约、收款、设置及你选择的照片。CSV 账本和 TXT 草稿也可能包含个人或租赁资料。这些导出文件是可读文件，存放位置由你通过系统“文件”界面选择。若选择云端文件提供商，文件按该提供商的设置与政策处理；开发者不会因此收到文件。

你可以修改记录、删除未关联租约的房源、撤销收款或删除单独提醒。部分记录为保持账本关系不能直接单独删除。要移除全部 iOS 本机资料，请在确认需要的备份已妥善保存后删除应用；“卸载 App”保留资料的系统选项不等同于“删除 App”。已导出的备份及其他副本需要你在相应位置另行删除。应用没有云账户，也没有供开发者代为恢复或删除的租客数据库。

网页、支持邮件与 Apple 服务

主动打开在线支持或隐私页面时，浏览器会访问网站托管商。使用 GitHub Pages 托管的页面可能由 GitHub 按其隐私声明处理 IP 地址、请求时间等访问记录；网页不嵌入广告、统计脚本或第三方字体。应用本地功能不依赖此网站。

如果你主动发送支持邮件，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和附件，仅用于答复、排查问题和必要的支持记录。邮件由邮件服务商处理。请尽量使用虚构资料或打码截图，不发送真实租客资料、密码或完整备份。支持记录在解决问题及必要后续处理期间保留；你可通过上述邮箱申请删除开发者控制范围内、已不再需要且没有保留义务的支持记录。

Apple 的 App Store、TestFlight、系统诊断和你主动提交的测试反馈按 Apple 的政策与设备设置运行。通过 TestFlight 提交的反馈、截图或诊断可能提供给开发者用于修复问题；请避免包含真实租客资料。

你的选择与未来更新

你可在系统设置中关闭通知；iOS 版不申请相机或相册访问权限。不提供照片时仍可使用内置封面，不允许通知时仍可使用本机账本。应用面向房东，不属于儿童专用服务。家庭模板中的人数仅为可编辑的填写起点，不向开发者收集儿童资料。若未来加入联网服务、账号或新的数据处理方式，我们会更新此政策和相应商店隐私说明。

Rental records stay on your device

Effective 11 October 2026. Developer: Wenjie Lu. Contact: luwenjie.nz@gmail.com.

RentDesk is an offline tool for property, lease, rent and reminder records. It has no account system, cloud sync, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. It does not upload property details, tenant records, rent records, photos or usage records to the developer.

Information processed locally

You may enter property names and addresses, city and area, rooms and occupancy, reference rent and notes; tenant names and contact details, adult/child counts, lease dates and recurring rent; payment amounts, dates and notes; and reminder times, sound preferences and reminder text. This information is used locally to organize records, calculate balances, prepare drafts and schedule local notifications. The app does not read contacts or location, connect to banks or process payments.

Photos you actively select are decoded, resized and re-encoded on the device. They are not automatically uploaded. Built-in covers are generic AI-generated illustrations included with the app. The iOS app imports only a JPEG, PNG or WebP you select in the system Files picker. It does not request camera or photo-library permission and has no camera capture entry. Save an existing Photos image to Files before importing it. Daily and rent notifications use generic text; custom reminder text can appear on the lock screen. Notification, sound and preview settings remain under your control in iOS.

Retention, backups and deletion

The iOS app stores records in its private application directory with system file protection, retaining the previous valid record for failure protection. The data directory is excluded from automatic application backup. Device installations have independent records and there is no iCloud sync. Export JSON backups regularly, particularly before changing devices or deleting the app.

JSON backups include properties, tenant contact details, leases, payments, settings and selected property photos. CSV ledgers and TXT drafts may also contain personal or rental information. Exports are readable files. You choose their destination in the system Files interface. A cloud file provider you choose processes files under its own policies; the developer does not receive those files.

You can edit records, delete properties without linked leases, undo payments and delete individual reminders. Some records cannot be deleted individually because they preserve ledger relationships. To remove all local iOS app records, delete the app after safely keeping any backup you need; the iOS Offload App option retains documents and is different from Delete App. Delete exported backups and other copies separately from their chosen locations. There is no cloud account or developer-held tenant database to delete or restore.

Web pages, email support and Apple services

Opening an online support or privacy page connects your browser to the hosting provider. GitHub Pages may process request information such as IP address and time under the GitHub privacy statement. These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or third-party fonts. The local app workflow does not depend on the website.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and attachments to respond, investigate issues and maintain necessary support records. Email is processed by the email provider. Prefer fictional data or redacted screenshots and avoid sending real tenant information, passwords or complete backups. Support records are retained while needed to resolve the issue and necessary follow-up. You may request deletion of records within the developer's control that are no longer needed and are not subject to retention obligations.

Apple distribution, TestFlight, system diagnostics and feedback you choose to submit operate under Apple's policies and your settings. TestFlight feedback, screenshots or diagnostics may be made available to the developer for fixing issues. Avoid including real tenant information in feedback.

Your choices and future updates

You can turn off notifications in system settings. The iOS app does not request camera or photo-library access. Built-in covers remain available without selecting a photo, and the ledger remains usable without notifications. The app is designed for landlords, not as a children's service. Household counts are editable template values; the developer does not collect children's records. New accounts, online services or data-processing practices would require updates to this policy and the applicable store disclosures.
