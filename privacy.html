出租资料，保存在自己的设备上

适用于 RentDesk 0.2.2。更新日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日。开发者：Wenjie Lu。联系邮箱：luwenjie.nz@gmail.com。

RentDesk 租屋管家是一款离线房源、租约、租金和提醒记录工具。应用不设账号，不提供云同步，不接入广告、跟踪或第三方分析软件包，也不将房源、租客、租金、照片或使用记录上传给开发者。

极简和高级模式均免费，共用本机资料，不需要订阅或付费解锁。切换模式只改变显示的功能入口，不删除已有记录。你主动导出文件、发送支持邮件或打开外部地图时，会按下文说明交出相应内容。

应用在本机处理哪些资料

  • 你输入的房源名称、地址、城市、区域、房型、卧室、人数、参考租金与备注，用于整理房源和生成文案草稿。
  • 你输入的租客姓名、可选的电话、邮箱及其他联系方式、成人与儿童人数、租约及入住搬离日期和周期租金，用于登记租约、显示入住状态和计算应收记录。应用不读取通讯录或设备定位。
  • 你登记的收款金额、日期和备注，用于显示收款与余额及生成所选租约的账单。应用不连接银行，不执行支付。
  • 你主动选择的房屋照片在本机解码、缩小并重新编码后保存。应用不自动上传图片；默认封面是随应用提供的通用 AI 示意图。iOS 的房源封面通过系统“文件”选择 JPEG、PNG 或 WebP，相册照片可先存到文件再导入。
  • 你选中的合同 PDF 和照片，以及入住或搬离检查中的房间、房况、钥匙数量、水表读数、备注和照片，保存在本机。iOS 仅在你主动选择合同或交接检查的拍照功能时请求相机权限，不请求相册权限；已有文件仍可通过系统“文件”选择。PDF 原件及照片中的内容由你决定，请避免保存无关个人资料。
  • 每日时间、租金提前天数、声音、租约及保修事项提醒设置和单独提醒内容，供本机安排系统通知。每日、租金和租约事项提醒使用通用提示；你填写的单独提醒内容可能出现在锁屏通知中。可在 iOS 设置中关闭通知、声音或通知预览。切回极简模式不会自动关闭已启用的提醒。

保存、备份与删除

iOS 版在应用专属目录保存资料，并使用系统文件保护；保存时保留上一份有效记录用于故障保护。本机资料目录排除在应用自动备份之外。各设备资料独立，没有 iCloud 同步，请通过“设置与备份”定期导出 JSON，迁移设备或删除应用前尤其需要备份。

JSON 完整备份包含房源、租客联系方式、租约及合同附件、收款、维修记录、交接检查及照片、提醒和设置。合同附件、交接 PDF、租客账单 PDF、CSV 账本和 TXT 草稿也可能包含个人或租赁资料。这些导出文件可读，应用不为它们设置密码或加密。保存位置由你选择；iOS 使用系统“文件”界面。若选择云端文件提供商，文件按该提供商的设置与政策处理；开发者不会因此收到文件。

你可以修改记录、删除未关联租约或维修履历的房源、撤销收款或删除单独提醒。部分记录为保持资料关系不能直接单独删除。要移除全部 iOS 本机资料，请在确认需要的备份已妥善保存后删除应用；“卸载 App”保留资料的系统选项不等同于“删除 App”。已导出的备份及其他副本需要你在相应位置另行删除。应用没有云账户，也没有供开发者代为恢复或删除的租客数据库。

维修履历与主动交接

应用在本机保存房屋维修问题、部位、材料型号、费用、施工方、日期、保修信息、前后照片及完工记录。记录关联房屋，不会因租客更换自动删除。完整 JSON 备份也包含这些维修记录和照片。

只有在你主动导出和发送维修资料时，所选内容才交给你选择的接收方。维修资料包按固定字段导出选定维修及必要房屋位置，不自动包含租客身份、租约、欠租、收租记录、工资或其他房屋。自行填写的说明或照片仍可能含个人信息，请分享前检查。资料文件可读、未加密，接收方能够保存副本；应用不能撤回对方副本。接收或发送文件不等于开发者收集该文件，应用没有自动上传、云同步或自动向施工方发消息。

交接检查与租客账单报告

高级模式可在本机生成所选入住或搬离检查的 PDF，选择含照片或仅文字版本；报告含房源名称、位置、检查日期、房况、钥匙、水表和你填写的备注。租客账单 PDF 包含所选房源及租客姓名、期间应收与实收、余额和相关收款备注。报告不会自动发送。请核对内容后再用自己的工具分享；接收方可以保存副本，应用不能撤回。未导出的本机照片仍随完整备份保存。

由你主动打开外部地图

只有点击“地图”或“打开地图”时，RentDesk 才把该入口使用的地址、城市及区域文字作为搜索内容交给外部地图：iOS 使用 Apple Maps，Android、Windows 和浏览器版使用 Google Maps。不会附带租客姓名、联系方式、租约、欠租、照片或其他房源记录。没有填写位置时，仅打开通用地图入口。请勿把租客等无关资料写入地址字段。

RentDesk 不读取设备 GPS、不申请定位权限，也不接收地图的当前位置或搜索结果。外部地图应用或网页可能联网，并按其自身权限、账号设置及隐私政策处理搜索和访问资料。如果在地图中使用“当前位置”或导航，其定位权限由地图应用、浏览器和系统管理。可查看 Apple Maps 隐私说明及 Google 隐私政策。不打开地图仍可使用本机房源和账本。

网页、支持邮件与 Apple 服务

主动打开在线支持或隐私页面时，浏览器会访问网站托管商。使用 GitHub Pages 托管的页面可能由 GitHub 按其隐私声明处理 IP 地址、请求时间等访问记录；网页不嵌入广告、统计脚本或第三方字体。应用本地功能不依赖此网站。

如果你主动发送支持邮件，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容和附件，仅用于答复、排查问题和必要的支持记录。邮件由邮件服务商处理。请尽量使用虚构资料或打码截图，不发送真实租客资料、密码或完整备份。支持记录在解决问题及必要后续处理期间保留；你可通过上述邮箱申请删除开发者控制范围内、已不再需要且没有保留义务的支持记录。

Apple 的 App Store、TestFlight、系统诊断和你主动提交的测试反馈按 Apple 的政策与设备设置运行。通过 TestFlight 提交的反馈、截图或诊断可能提供给开发者用于修复问题；请避免包含真实租客资料。

你的选择与未来更新

你可在系统设置中关闭通知，以及合同或交接拍照的相机权限。应用不要求访问相册。不提供照片时仍可使用内置封面，不允许通知时仍可使用本机账本。应用面向房东，不属于儿童专用服务。家庭模板中的人数仅为可编辑的填写起点，不向开发者收集儿童资料。若未来加入账号或新的数据处理方式，我们会更新此政策和相应商店隐私说明。

Rental records stay on your device

For RentDesk 0.2.2. Updated 7 November 2026. Developer: Wenjie Lu. Contact: luwenjie.nz@gmail.com.

RentDesk is an offline tool for property, lease, rent and reminder records. It has no account system, cloud sync, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDK. It does not upload property details, tenant records, rent records, photos or usage records to the developer.

Simple and Advanced modes are both free, use the same local records and require no subscription or paid unlock. Switching modes changes the visible controls without deleting records. Exporting files, sending support email and opening external maps are optional actions that disclose the content described below.

Information processed locally

You may enter property names and addresses, city and area, rooms and occupancy, reference rent and notes; tenant names, optional phone, email and other contact details, adult/child counts, lease and move-in/move-out dates and recurring rent; payment amounts, dates and notes; and reminder settings and text. This information is used locally to organize records, show occupancy, calculate balances, prepare drafts and statements, and schedule local notifications. The app does not read contacts or device location, connect to banks or process payments.

Photos you actively select are decoded, resized and re-encoded on the device, without automatic upload. Built-in covers are generic AI-generated illustrations. On iOS, property covers are imported as JPEG, PNG or WebP through Files; an image in Photos can first be saved to Files. Leases can include PDF and photo attachments. Move-in and move-out inspections can include room condition, key counts, water readings, notes and photos. On iOS, camera permission is requested only when you choose to photograph a contract or an inspection. Photo-library permission is not requested, and existing files remain available through Files. Original PDFs and photo contents are chosen by you; avoid unnecessary personal information.

Daily, rent and lease-event reminders use generic text. Your custom reminder text can appear on the lock screen. Notification, sound and preview settings remain under your control in iOS. Switching back to Simple mode does not automatically disable reminders that were already enabled.

Retention, backups and deletion

The iOS app stores records in its private application directory with system file protection, retaining the previous valid record for failure protection. The data directory is excluded from automatic application backup. Device installations have independent records and there is no iCloud sync. Export JSON backups regularly, particularly before changing devices or deleting the app.

Full JSON backups include properties, tenant contact details, leases and contract attachments, payments, repairs, inspections and photos, reminders and settings. Exported contract attachments, inspection PDFs, tenant-statement PDFs, CSV ledgers and TXT drafts may contain personal or rental information. The app does not password-protect or encrypt exported files. You choose the destination, through Files on iOS. A cloud file provider you choose processes files under its own policies; the developer does not receive those files.

You can edit records, delete properties with no linked leases or repairs, undo payments and delete individual reminders. Some records cannot be deleted individually because they preserve data relationships. To remove all local iOS app records, delete the app after safely keeping any backup you need; the iOS Offload App option retains documents and is different from Delete App. Delete exported backups and other copies separately from their chosen locations. There is no cloud account or developer-held tenant database to delete or restore.

Inspection and tenant-statement reports

Advanced mode generates PDFs locally for a selected move-in or move-out inspection, with photos or text only. These reports include the property name and location, inspection date, room condition, keys, water readings and your notes. A tenant-statement PDF includes the selected property and tenant name, charges, receipts, balances and relevant payment notes. Reports are not sent automatically. Review them before sharing through your own tools. Recipients can retain copies that the app cannot recall. Local photos that you do not export remain part of a full backup.

External maps opened by you

Only when you choose Map or Open Map does RentDesk pass the address, city and area text used by that control to an external map search: Apple Maps on iOS, and Google Maps on Android, Windows and the browser version. Tenant names, contacts, leases, arrears, photos and other property records are not attached. A blank location opens the general map entry point. Do not put unrelated personal information in address fields.

RentDesk does not read device GPS, request location permission or receive map search results or your current location. The external map app or website may connect to the internet and process searches and requests under its own permissions, account settings and privacy policy. Current-location controls and navigation use permissions managed by that map app, browser and operating system. See Apple Maps privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy. Local records and the ledger remain usable without opening maps.

Web pages, email support and Apple services

Opening an online support or privacy page connects your browser to the hosting provider. GitHub Pages may process request information such as IP address and time under the GitHub privacy statement. These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts or third-party fonts. The local app workflow does not depend on the website.

If you email support, the developer receives your email address, message and attachments to respond, investigate issues and maintain necessary support records. Email is processed by the email provider. Prefer fictional data or redacted screenshots and avoid sending real tenant information, passwords or complete backups. Support records are retained while needed to resolve the issue and necessary follow-up. You may request deletion of records within the developer's control that are no longer needed and are not subject to retention obligations.

Apple distribution, TestFlight, system diagnostics and feedback you choose to submit operate under Apple's policies and your settings. TestFlight feedback, screenshots or diagnostics may be made available to the developer for fixing issues. Avoid including real tenant information in feedback.

Your choices and future updates

You can turn off notifications and camera access for contracts or inspections in system settings. Selecting existing files remains available. Built-in covers remain available without selecting a photo, and the ledger remains usable without notifications. The app is designed for landlords, not as a children's service. Household counts are editable template values; the developer does not collect children's records. New accounts or data-processing practices would require updates to this policy and the applicable store disclosures.

Maintenance records and voluntary handover

The app stores repair issues, locations, materials, model numbers, costs, contractors, dates, warranty information, photos and completion history locally, associated with the property even when tenants change. Full JSON backups include these records. A repair handover file contains only the selected repair and necessary property identification/location; tenant, lease, rent arrears, payment, payroll and unrelated property records are not automatically exported. Check user-written text and photos for personal information before sharing. Files are readable and unencrypted, and recipients can retain copies. You choose and send files using your own tools; the app does not upload them to the developer, synchronize them to a server or automatically contact contractors.
