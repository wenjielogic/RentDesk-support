第一次使用

  1. 在“我的房源”选择单人、两人、一家三口、一家四口或合租模板。
  2. 填写房源名称、位置、房间、可住人数和参考每周租金，选择封面并保存。
  3. 在“租客与租约”登记实际租客、人数、租金周期、首次应收日和结束日期。
  4. 到“闹铃与提醒”允许系统通知，设置时间，然后主动发送一条测试提醒。
  5. 收到钱后，在“收租账本”点“记收款”。定期到“设置与备份”导出完整 JSON。

照片与家庭模板

👨‍👩‍👧 一家三口、👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 一家四口等模板只是快速填写起点，人数、位置和租金都需要按实际修改。内置独栋、公寓和单间图片是 AI 生成的通用示意图，不代表实际出租房屋。

点房源上的“换封面”，选择内置图，或点“从文件选择照片”导入已有 JPG、PNG、WebP。iOS 版不直接拍照或打开相册；相册照片可先使用系统“存储到文件”，再导入。图片在本机压缩，输入不超过 12 MiB／4000 万像素，保存后单张不超过 220 KiB、长边不超过 1280 像素。HEIC 等不支持的文件可先导出为 JPG。整个资料及完整备份上限为 5 MiB；超限时应用会拒绝本次保存并提示。

提醒为什么没有声音

iPhone 使用普通系统本地通知，不是会持续响铃的系统闹钟。先在 iOS 设置中检查 RentDesk 通知、声音、静音开关、音量及专注模式。点“发送一条测试提醒”后锁屏，等待约 5 秒检查结果。单独提醒需要设在未来时间，过去时间不会补响。

自动租金提醒滚动安排未来 30 天，请至少每月打开一次应用。最多安排最近 60 条单次通知，另加每日重复提醒；超过时页面会提示。修改设置或登记收款后会重新排程。虚构示例空间不安排真实定时提醒，但主动点击测试通知仍可用于检查设备。

收款、租约和催租

“记收款”只记录你已通过其他方式收到的钱，不会从租客账户扣款。支持部分收款；登记错误可撤销。月租按首次应收日计算，例如 1 月 31 日、2 月最后一天、3 月 31 日。结束日包含当天应收，不自动按天折算。同一房源的租约不能重叠，合租的独立房间可分别登记为房源。

已经创建的租约，其租金、周期和首次应收日不能直接改。涨租或重新出租时先结束旧租约，再建立新租约。“催租”和“招租文案”仅生成草稿，由你核对后自行发送或发布。应用不托管押金、不连接银行，也不生成租赁法律通知或合同。

备份、恢复和换手机

各设备的数据独立，没有账号或云同步。iOS 本机资料排除在应用自动备份之外；请主动导出 JSON，并在“文件”中确认已经保存。JSON 包含照片及完整资料，CSV 仅为账本，不能替代完整备份。恢复 JSON 会替换当前整个工作空间，按提示先导出当前资料。

删除 App 会移除本机资料，开发者没有云端副本可恢复。遇到读取错误时先保留应用和原有文件，不要卸载。备份是可读文件，请保存在自己控制的位置，不向无关人员发送。

体验虚构示例

只有空白空间可以载入虚构示例。示例内新增资料也属于示例空间，清空时会一起移除；清空前应用会要求导出备份。测试真实排程提醒时应在非示例空间创建虚构测试记录。

Getting started

In My Properties (我的房源), select a household template, enter the property location, occupancy and reference weekly rent, then choose a cover. Add the actual tenant and lease in Tenants and Leases (租客与租约). Set notification permission and times in Alarms and Reminders (闹铃与提醒). Record money received elsewhere in Rent Ledger (收租账本). Regularly export a full JSON backup from Settings and Backup (设置与备份).

Templates and photos

Single-person, two-person, family-of-three, family-of-four and shared-accommodation templates are editable starting points. Built-in house, apartment and bedroom images are generic AI-generated illustrations, not real listings. Choose an existing JPEG, PNG or WebP with the property editor’s Files button. The iOS app has no direct camera or photo-library entry; save an image from Photos to Files before importing it. Input limits are 12 MiB and 40 million pixels. Photos are compressed locally to at most 220 KiB and a 1280-pixel long edge. Convert unsupported formats such as HEIC to JPEG first. The complete dataset and backup are limited to 5 MiB; an over-limit save is refused without replacing previous records.

Notification checks

iOS reminders are ordinary local notifications, not continuously ringing alarms. Check app notifications, sound, silent mode, volume and Focus in iOS settings. Request a test notification, lock the device and wait about five seconds. Dated reminders must be in the future. Automatic rent reminders cover the next 30 days; open the app at least monthly. Up to 60 single notifications are scheduled, plus a repeating daily notification. Sample mode suppresses automatic notifications but permits an explicitly requested test.

Payments and leases

Payment entries record money received outside the app. Partial payments and undo are supported. Monthly schedules follow the original due day, using the last day for shorter months. The lease end date includes that day's rent and no daily proration is calculated. Leases for one property cannot overlap; separately rented rooms can be separate properties. End an existing lease and create a new one to change recurring rent, frequency or first due date. Reminder and listing text are editable drafts that you send or publish yourself. The app does not process payments, hold deposits or create legal notices or contracts.

Backups and moving devices

Each installation has separate records with no account or cloud sync. The iOS data directory is excluded from automatic application backup. Export JSON and verify it in Files. JSON includes photos and complete records; CSV is only a ledger and is not a full backup. Importing a backup replaces the workspace, so export current records first. Deleting the app removes its local records; the developer has no cloud copy to restore. If the app reports a read error, preserve the installation and original files instead of uninstalling. Exports are readable files that you should keep in a location you control.

Sample data

Fictional samples can be loaded only into an empty workspace. New records added in sample mode are also removed when that workspace is cleared; export a backup first. Use fictional records in a non-sample workspace to test scheduled notifications.
