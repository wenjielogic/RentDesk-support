第一次使用

  1. 默认进入极简模式。在“我的房源”点“添加房源”，或点“从房源模板开始”选择可编辑模板。
  2. 填写房源名称、具体位置和参考每周租金并保存。
  3. 在“租客与租约”登记实际租客、租金周期、首次应收日和结束日期。
  4. 到“闹铃与提醒”允许系统通知，设置时间，然后主动发送一条测试提醒。
  5. 收到钱后，在“收租账本”点“记收款”。定期到“设置与备份”导出完整 JSON。

极简与高级模式都免费

在右上角点“高级”展开更多功能，点“极简”收起；也可在“设置与备份”切换。0.2.2 的两个模式均免费，没有订阅或付费解锁，共用同一份本机资料。高级模式显示封面与详细房源资料、可选联系方式和合同附件、维修履历、入住与搬离检查、续约、租客账单及更多提醒设置。切回极简只隐藏这些入口，不删除已有内容，也不会自动关闭已开启的提醒；需要调整时可再切回高级。

用地址打开地图

房源卡片上的“地图”和编辑表单中的“打开地图”会打开外部地图。表单中使用当前填写的位置，房源卡片使用已保存的位置；iOS 使用 Apple Maps，Android、Windows 和浏览器版使用 Google Maps。没有填写位置时打开通用地图，可在地图中自行搜索或使用其当前位置功能。

点击后会把地址、城市和区域文字交给地图服务，不附带租客、租约或收款资料。RentDesk 不申请定位权限，也不会从地图取回当前位置或自动填写地址。外部地图可能需要网络，其定位和隐私设置由地图应用或浏览器控制。找不到位置时请检查地址，或回到 RentDesk 继续手动填写。详见隐私政策。

照片与家庭模板

👨‍👩‍👧 一家三口、👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 一家四口等模板只是快速填写起点，人数、位置和租金都需要按实际修改。内置独栋、公寓和单间图片是 AI 生成的通用示意图，不代表实际出租房屋。

切到高级模式，点房源上的“换封面”，选择内置图，或点“从文件选择照片”导入已有 JPG、PNG、WebP。iOS 的房源封面通过系统“文件”导入，相册照片可先使用“存储到文件”。房源封面、维修和交接照片在本机压缩，输入不超过 12 MiB／4000 万像素，保存后单张不超过 220 KiB、长边不超过 1280 像素。合同照片使用单独的附件大小限制。HEIC 等不支持的文件可先导出为 JPG。整个资料及完整备份上限为 32 MiB；超限时应用会拒绝本次保存并提示。

提醒为什么没有声音

iPhone 使用普通系统本地通知，不是会持续响铃的系统闹钟。先在 iOS 设置中检查 RentDesk 通知、声音、静音开关、音量及专注模式。点“发送一条测试提醒”后锁屏，等待约 5 秒检查结果。单独提醒需要设在未来时间，过去时间不会补响。

自动租金提醒滚动安排未来 30 天，请至少每月打开一次应用。最多安排最近 60 条单次通知，另加每日重复提醒；超过时页面会提示。修改设置或登记收款后会重新排程。虚构示例空间不安排真实定时提醒，但主动点击测试通知仍可用于检查设备。

高级模式还可查看即将到来的入住、租约结束、搬离和维修保修事项，并在提醒页主动开启相关提醒。日期是你的管理记录，不是法律通知期限或保修承诺。切换显示模式不会自动暂停这些提醒。

收款、租约和催租

“记收款”只记录你已通过其他方式收到的钱，不会从租客账户扣款。支持部分收款；登记错误可撤销。月租按首次应收日计算，例如 1 月 31 日、2 月最后一天、3 月 31 日。结束日包含当天应收，不自动按天折算。同一房源的租约不能重叠，合租的独立房间可分别登记为房源。

已经创建的租约，其租金、周期和首次应收日不能直接改。涨租或重新出租时先结束旧租约，再建立新租约。高级模式的“登记续约”会带入原租客和租金，请先填写旧租约结束日，再核对新租约的首次应收日和租金；旧账和合同仍保留。“催租”和高级模式中的“招租文案”仅生成草稿，由你核对后自行发送或发布。应用不托管押金、不连接银行，也不生成租赁法律通知或合同。

在高级模式打开对应租约，可填写入住和搬离日期、电话、邮箱和其他联系方式。联系方式可以留空。房源入住状态优先使用入住、搬离日期，缺少日期时会标为推算；这些日期不会自动改动租金周期或按天折算。要停止后续计租，请核对租约结束日；搬离后已有欠租仍保留。

在“租房合同”选取 PDF 或已有照片，也可以在手机上点“拍照保存”。保存租约后，附件留在本机，可单独导出，也会包含在完整备份里。每份租约最多 8 个附件，单份 PDF 最多 5 MiB。合同附件用于保存已有文件，应用不生成或签署法律合同。

入住与搬离检查、租客账单

在高级模式的租约卡片点“入住 / 退租交接”，分别记录入住和搬离检查。可选择房间模板，填写各项房况、钥匙数量、水表读数和备注，并对照两次检查。每个房间最多 4 张照片，每份检查最多 24 张；iOS 可选已有照片或主动拍照，其他平台通过照片文件选择。保存后可导出“含照片 PDF”或“文字 PDF”，照片仍保存在本机和完整备份中。报告仅反映记录内容，不自动判断责任或扣款。

在租约卡片或收租账本点“租客账单”，选择租约及日期范围，预览期初余额、期间应收和实收、期末余额与待收记录后导出 PDF。它反映手工账目，不是银行到账证明。PDF 在本机生成，单份上限 5 MiB；内容过多时可缩短账单日期范围，交接报告可改选文字版。检查文件后再自行发送，应用不会自动联系租客。

房屋维修履历与资料交接

切到高级模式，在“我的房源”点“维修履历”，或打开“房屋维修”，选房子后登记哪里坏了、维修前后照片、材料型号、费用、施工方和保修日期。记录跟着房子保存，换租客不会清空。每阶段最多 4 张照片；保修日期仅作记录，请按实际约定核对。

选择一条维修记录，预览后导出维修资料文件，使用你自己的聊天或邮件工具交给施工方。对方可在新版 RentDesk 的维修页面导入请求、填写完工资料并导出回执；房东导入回执，核对后保存到原房屋履历。请使用维修资料文件，不要把完整工作空间备份发给施工方。

维修资料仅含选定维修及必要房屋位置，不自动包含租客、租约、欠租、工资或其他房屋。分享前仍要检查自己填写的自由文本和照片中是否有无关个人信息。单个资料包上限 5 MiB，重复或过期回执会提示。TradeDesk／SiteLog 的接收端需要支持同一维修资料格式；现有旧版本没有自动同步功能。

备份、恢复和换手机

各设备的数据独立，没有账号或云同步。iOS 本机资料排除在应用自动备份之外；请主动导出 JSON，并在“文件”中确认已经保存。两个模式导出的完整 JSON 都包含已有合同附件、维修、交接检查及照片；CSV 和 PDF 报告不能替代完整备份。恢复 JSON 会替换当前整个工作空间，按提示先导出当前资料。

删除 App 会移除本机资料，开发者没有云端副本可恢复。遇到读取错误时先保留应用和原有文件，不要卸载。备份是可读文件，请保存在自己控制的位置，不向无关人员发送。

只有空白空间可以载入虚构示例。示例内新增资料也属于示例空间，清空时会一起移除；清空前应用会要求导出备份。测试真实排程提醒时应在非示例空间创建虚构测试记录。

Getting started

The app starts in Simple mode. In My Properties (我的房源), choose Add Property or From a Property Template, enter the name, address and reference weekly rent, then save. Add the actual tenant, rent frequency and due dates in Tenants and Leases (租客与租约). Set notification permission and times in Alarms and Reminders (闹铃与提醒). Record money received elsewhere in Rent Ledger (收租账本). Regularly export a full JSON backup from Settings and Backup (设置与备份).

Both modes are free

Use the top-right Advanced (高级) or Simple (极简) button, or the mode controls in Settings and Backup. Both modes in 0.2.2 are free, with no subscription or paid unlock, and share the same local records. Advanced mode exposes cover editing and property details, optional contacts and contract attachments, repairs, inspections, renewals, tenant statements and more reminder settings. Returning to Simple hides these controls without deleting records or disabling existing reminders. Switch back to Advanced when you need to change them.

Open a map with the property address

Map on a property card uses its saved location. Open Map in the editor uses the location currently entered in the form. These controls open Apple Maps on iOS and Google Maps on Android, Windows and the browser version. A blank location opens the general map, where you can search or use that map's current-location controls.

A tap passes the address, city and area text to the map service, without tenant, lease or payment records. RentDesk requests no location permission, receives no current-location result and does not automatically fill the address. External maps may need internet access and manage their own privacy and location permissions. Check the address if it is not found, or continue entering it manually in RentDesk. See the privacy policy.

Templates and photos

Single-person, two-person, family-of-three, family-of-four and shared-accommodation templates are editable starting points. Built-in house, apartment and bedroom images are generic AI-generated illustrations, not real listings. In Advanced mode, choose Change Cover or an existing JPEG, PNG or WebP with the property editor’s Files button. On iOS, save an image from Photos to Files before importing it as a cover. Property, repair and inspection photos have input limits of 12 MiB and 40 million pixels, and are compressed locally to at most 220 KiB and a 1280-pixel long edge. Contract photos have separate attachment limits. Convert unsupported formats such as HEIC to JPEG first. The complete dataset and backup are limited to 32 MiB. If a save exceeds that limit, the app keeps the previous records.

Notification checks

iOS reminders are ordinary local notifications, not continuously ringing alarms. Check app notifications, sound, silent mode, volume and Focus in iOS settings. Request a test notification, lock the device and wait about five seconds. Dated reminders must be in the future. Automatic rent reminders cover the next 30 days; open the app at least monthly. Up to 60 single notifications are scheduled, plus a repeating daily notification. Sample mode suppresses automatic notifications but permits an explicitly requested test.

Advanced mode also shows upcoming move-ins, lease ends, move-outs and repair warranty dates, with optional reminders enabled in the reminder settings. These are your management records, not legal notice deadlines or warranty promises. Switching modes does not automatically pause existing reminders.

Payments and leases

Payment entries record money received outside the app. Partial payments and undo are supported. Monthly schedules follow the original due day, using the last day for shorter months. The lease end date includes that day's rent and no daily proration is calculated. Leases for one property cannot overlap; separately rented rooms can be separate properties. End an existing lease and create a new one to change recurring rent, frequency or first due date. Advanced mode's Register Renewal carries over the tenant and rent after you set the old lease's end date; check the new first due date and rent before saving. Old records and contracts remain. Rent-reminder and listing text are drafts that you send or publish yourself; listing drafts are in Advanced mode. The app does not process payments, hold deposits or create legal notices or contracts.

In Advanced mode, open a lease to enter move-in and move-out dates and optional phone, email or other contact details. Occupancy uses move dates where available and marks inferred status when dates are missing. Move dates do not automatically change the rent schedule or prorate rent. Check the lease end date to stop future rent charges; previous arrears remain after a move-out.

In Lease Contract, select a PDF or existing photo, or use the phone camera to photograph a page. Save the lease to keep the attachments on your device. You can export them individually or include them in a full backup. Each lease can hold up to eight attachments, and each PDF can be up to 5 MiB. These attachments store existing documents; the app does not create or sign legal contracts.

Move-in/out inspections and tenant statements

In Advanced mode, choose Move-in / Move-out Handover (入住 / 退租交接) on a lease card. Record room conditions, keys, water readings, notes and photos, then compare the two inspections. Each room supports up to four photos and each inspection up to 24. iOS offers selected files or an explicitly requested camera capture; other platforms use a photo file picker. Save before exporting a PDF with photos or text only. Photos remain in local records and full backups. Reports reflect recorded observations without automatically deciding liability or deductions.

Choose Tenant Statement (租客账单) from a lease or the ledger, select the lease and date range, preview opening balance, charges, receipts, closing balance and outstanding entries, then export a PDF. It reflects manual records, not bank confirmation. PDFs are generated locally with a 5 MiB limit. Reduce the date range for a large statement, or choose a text-only inspection report. Review files before sharing them yourself; the app does not automatically contact tenants.

Backups and moving devices

Each installation has separate records with no account or cloud sync. The iOS data directory is excluded from automatic application backup. Export JSON and verify it in Files. Full backups from either mode include existing contract attachments, repairs, inspections and photos. CSV and PDF reports are not full backups. Importing a backup replaces the workspace, so export current records first. Deleting the app removes its local records; the developer has no cloud copy to restore. If the app reports a read error, preserve the installation and original files instead of uninstalling. Exports are readable files that you should keep in a location you control.

Sample data

Fictional samples can be loaded only into an empty workspace. New records added in sample mode are also removed when that workspace is cleared; export a backup first. Use fictional records in a non-sample workspace to test scheduled notifications.

Property maintenance history

Switch to Advanced mode and open Property Repairs or the property's repair-history button. Records include locations, before/after photos, materials and model numbers, costs, contractors and warranty dates. Each phase supports up to four photos. Tenant changes do not remove property history. Export one selected repair request, review it, and share the file yourself. Another updated RentDesk installation can import the request and return a completion file; review incoming results before adding them to the original record. Repair files exclude tenant, lease, arrears, payroll and unrelated property records. Check your own free text and photos before sharing. Each repair file is limited to 5 MiB; the complete workspace is limited to 32 MiB. TradeDesk and SiteLog receivers must support the same interchange format; older apps do not gain automatic synchronization.
